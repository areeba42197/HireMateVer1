Core Features

Everything you need to land the role

From discovery to outreach — HireMate handles the heavy lifting while you stay in control.

Smart Lead Discovery
AI scans LinkedIn posts and identifies genuine hiring opportunities matched to your skills and preferences — no more endless scrolling.
Lead Prioritization
Every lead is ranked Hot, Warm, or Cold based on relevance, post engagement, and recency — so you always focus on what matters most.
AI Draft Messages
Get professional, personalized message and comment drafts for every lead. Edit them your way before sending — nothing goes out without your approval.
Ethical Filtering
All AI-generated drafts are checked for professionalism and filtered for spammy or inappropriate content before they ever reach you.
Insights Dashboard
Track your lead activity, response rates, and engagement trends in a clean analytics dashboard designed to help you improve over time.
Full User Control
HireMate is assistance, not automation. Every message, comment, and action requires your explicit approval — your LinkedIn, your rules.
How It Works

Simple. Smart. Ethical.

1
Create your profile
