How to connect LinkedIn
This lets HireMate find opportunities from your signed-in LinkedIn account. Your session cookie value is encrypted and saved securely.
1. Open LinkedIn in Chrome or Edge and make sure you are logged in.
2. Press F12, or right click the page and choose Inspect.
3. Open the Application tab. If you do not see it, click the >> menu.
4. On the left, open Cookies, then choose https://www.linkedin.com.
5. Find the row named li_at. Copy its Value.
6. Paste that value below and click Save LinkedIn session cookie.
HireMate only shows a hidden version after saving, so your session cookie stays private.
