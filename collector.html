LinkedIn Post Collector

Server-side LinkedIn post scraping can return blocked or unreadable HTML even with a cookie. This collector runs inside your already logged-in LinkedIn tab, reads visible hiring posts, and saves them to HireMate.

🔒 The script sends only visible post text, visible counts, and visible post links to your localhost backend. It does not send your LinkedIn password or session cookie.
  1. Login to LinkedIn in your browser.
  2. Search LinkedIn posts for your target role, for example: frontend developer hiring.
  3. Open browser DevTools Console on LinkedIn.
  4. Paste the copied script and press Enter.
  5. Keep the LinkedIn tab open. It collects visible posts every 2 minutes while you scroll.
